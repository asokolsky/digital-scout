# Practical Problems

## Wash Sale

Q: A [wash sale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wash_sale) is a sale of securities "at a loss when substantially identical securities were acquired shortly before or after the date of sale."
Why is this illegal?

A: IRS said so.

## Equivalent Rate of Return

Q: Delaying social security from the age 67 to the age 70 results in about 24% benefits increase. What is the equivalent yearly rate of return?
Ignore the years of forgone payments, and the longevity break-even analysis.

A: 7.44%. Note also the benefit is also inflation-adjusted.

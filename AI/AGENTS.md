# AI Content Conventions

These instructions apply to files in this directory.

## Editorial Intent

- Keep `index.md` concise and practical rather than academically comprehensive.
- Preserve intentional simplifications unless they are materially inaccurate or
  misleading.
- Treat the high-level historical overview and the current section order as
  intentional. Do not recommend restructuring them solely to add completeness.

## Style

- Use `PROs` and `CONs` as comparison-table headings.
- Keep the cloud-hosted versus local-model comparison as a compact table.
- Prefer direct language and concrete examples over additional conceptual detail.

## Reviews

- Prioritize factual errors, contradictions, broken links, unclear language, and
  rendering problems.
- Do not repeat purely stylistic suggestions that conflict with the conventions
  above unless the file has become inconsistent or new evidence warrants a
  change.

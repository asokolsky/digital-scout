# Career and Job Search

Think of your career as a business: define what you offer, develop your skills,
communicate your value, and negotiate compensation.

You wear three hats:

- [Product](product.md) manager: define your professional identity and what you offer.
- [Developer](development.md): build your technical and soft skills.
- [Salesperson](sales.md): present your value to prospective employers.

Start with one area, then adjust your approach as you gain experience and feedback.

## Your Product Marketing Mix

Use the [marketing mix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing_mix) to organize your career decisions:

- [Product](product.md): your skills, experience, and professional identity.
- [Price](price.md): compensation for your role, level, and market.
- [Place/Distribution](place.md): geography, industry, and employer type.
- [Promotion](promotion.md): how you communicate your value.
